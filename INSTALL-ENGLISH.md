# My Niches — Easy install guide
My Niches — by Avantex (Usama javed)

It takes about 10 minutes. You need a Windows computer and Google Chrome.
There are 6 steps below. Start at the top and do them one by one.

## Step 1: Install Node.js (first time only)

This is a small program that My Niches runs on.

1. Open this link in Chrome: https://nodejs.org
2. Download using the button that says **LTS**.
3. Open the downloaded file. Click **Next** on every screen, then **Install**, then **Finish**.

## Step 2: Unzip the file

1. **Right-click** `my-niches.zip` and click **Extract All...**
2. Click **Browse...**, choose **Documents**, then click **Extract**.
3. A folder called `my-niches` appears in Documents.

Never delete or move this folder. The tool runs from it.

## Step 3: Start the tool

1. Open the `my-niches` folder.
2. Double-click **start.cmd**.
3. If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
4. If a "Security Warning" appears: click **Run**.

**MY NICHES** opens in your browser. Done!

A small black window called "My Niches server" also opens in the taskbar at the bottom. Do not close it.

## Step 4: Keep the tool always on

1. In the same `my-niches` folder, double-click **install-autostart.cmd**.
2. When it says "Done", press any key.

Now the tool starts by itself every time you turn on your computer. You do not need to run start.cmd again.

This is the link to open the tool. Bookmark it: http://localhost:5173

## Step 5: Add the Chrome extension

This puts a green **+** button next to the Subscribe button on YouTube.

1. In Chrome's address bar at the top, type `chrome://extensions` and press **Enter**.
2. At the top right, turn **on** the **Developer mode** switch.
3. At the top left, click **Load unpacked**.
4. Open the `my-niches` folder, choose the **extension** folder **inside** it, and click **Select Folder**.
5. Click the puzzle-shaped icon at the top of Chrome and click the pin next to **My Niches**.

## Step 6: Save your first channel

1. In My Niches, click the green **+ New niche** button, type a name (for example "Cooking") and save.
2. Open any channel on YouTube.
3. Click the green **+** next to Subscribe and choose your niche.

That's it! The channel's name, picture, subscribers and top videos appear by themselves.

Another way: open a niche in the app, click **+ Add channel**, and paste a YouTube channel link.

## If something goes wrong

- **The page does not open:** double-click start.cmd again.
- **The extension says "Cannot find the server":** double-click start.cmd.
- **It says "Node.js is not installed":** do Step 1.
- **A channel has no name or subscribers:** wait a little, then click the refresh button on that card.
- **You moved the folder:** run install-autostart.cmd again, then remove the extension from Chrome and do Step 5 again.

---

**Did someone give you a link and a password?** Then you do not need to install anything. Open the link, type the password, click **Unlock**. Then only do Step 5. When you click the green **+** on YouTube, a box appears: type the link and the password in it and click **Save and connect**.
